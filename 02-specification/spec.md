## 1. Overview

A table-top box, **190 × 160 × 126 mm**, split horizontally into two compartments (R2):

- **RPI part (top):** Raspberry Pi on the divider plate, temperature-sensor jack at the back.
- **MAINS part (bottom):** relay board hanging under the divider plate, USB-C power adapter on the bottom lid, 3 rocker switches at the front, IEC power inlet + 2 power sockets at the back.

Four printed parts (R16), all printed **without supports**, in one filament (R13):

| # | Part | `part=` | Size (mm) | Print orientation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Body (walls, no floor/ceiling) | `body` | 190 × 160 × 120 | upright, bottom edge on the bed |
| 2 | Divider plate | `divider` | 183.4 × 153.4 × 9 | **upside down** (standoffs pointing up) |
| 3 | Top lid | `top_lid` | 190 × 160 × 3.8 | outer face up (raised logo on top) |
| 4 | Bottom lid | `bottom_lid` | 190 × 160 × ~15 | outer face down (adapter holder up) |

Every part fits within 200 × 200 × 200 mm (R21).

## 2. Coordinate system

- Units: mm.
- Origin: **front-left-bottom outer corner of the body** (the bottom lid is below z = 0).
- +X to the right (seen from the front), +Y to the back, +Z up.
- Z levels: bottom lid −3…0 · MAINS part 0…70 · divider plate 70…73 · RPI part 73…120 · top lid 120…123.

## 8. FDM design-rule check

| Rule (docs/fdm-design-rules.md) | How it is met |
|---|---|
| 1 Flat build-plate face | Body: bottom edge. Divider: top face (printed upside down). Top lid: inner face. Bottom lid: outer face |
| 2 Overhangs ≤ 45° or bridged | Pads and panel bosses: 45° chamfers underneath. Rectangular cutouts: top edge bridges ≤ 28.5 mm. Thinning pocket: 1 mm step |
| 3 Horizontal holes teardrop / flat-top | Sockets: flat-top teardrop. Jack: teardrop. Vent slots: rounded vertical slots (3 mm bridge) |
| 4 Bottom edges chamfered | Body outer bottom edge 0.6 chamfer. Lids are flat plates |
| 5 No floating islands | All features grow from the bed face |
| 6 Walls | `wall` = 3, ribs/brackets ≥ 2 |
| 7 Mating fits | P9 `fit_clearance` |
| 9 Screw holes | M3/M2.5 heat-set inserts, Ø3.4 through holes |
| 10 Manifold | checked by `tools/check.sh` |

Smallest details: labels 6 mm text, slots 3 mm wide. No features below 2 mm (R13).

## 10. Assembly order (informative)

1. Press all inserts (body, divider, sockets/IEC bosses).
2. Snap the switches into the front. Screw the IEC inlet and sockets to the back. Fit the jack.
3. Divider: relay board underneath, Pi standoffs + Pi on top.
4. Wire the mains part, then lower the divider from the top onto the pads (relay board first) and screw it down. Route the GPIO and USB-C cables through the feed-through.
5. Bottom lid with adapter (cable tie), then screw it on. Feet. Top lid.
